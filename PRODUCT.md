# Product

## Platform
web
## Stack
React, approved by user; Vinext starter with Tailwind and existing Radix/shadcn components. Code-led implementation approved.
## Users
Crowdfunding participants, campaign owners and competition judges evaluating the concept.
## Product Purpose
Explain and demonstrate confidential all-or-nothing crowdfunding.
## Capabilities and Constraints
An owner sets a goal and deadline. During the campaign only aggregate percentage is public, individual amounts use ZK commitments in the proposed protocol. At deadline exactly reaching the goal releases funds; otherwise contributions are refunded privately. Public final result is binary. Frontend is an explicitly labelled simulation without a wallet, real funds or ZK implementation. Sample goal $100, duration five days, initial progress 65%. Reject contributions exceeding goal, as approved.
## Brand Commitments
The name Veil is explicitly excluded because it belongs to another project. Confirmed project name: Cairn. Turkish interface. Approved graphite, off-white and emerald palette, sans typography, light/dark themes, restrained motion, asymmetrical hero and interactive demo.
